*Website*: [obiapp.in](https://obiapp.in)

Turn a message from WhatsApp or Instagram into a tracked order, give your customer a link that shows where it is, and keep your materials, costs and profit straight.

Built for small, home-grown businesses that run on chat apps and spreadsheets. Started in August 2025.
